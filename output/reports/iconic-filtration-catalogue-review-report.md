# ICONIC FILTRATION Catalogue Review Report

## Review Scope

The complete catalogue was reviewed for generic marketing language, repeated wording, placeholder content, unsupported claims, website references, product-page completeness, technical clarity, image suitability, and B2B usefulness.

The revised catalogue contains 15 A4 pages and follows this flow:

1. Company Introduction
2. Why Choose ICONIC FILTRATION
3. Partner Brands
4. Product Categories
5. Individual Product Pages
6. Industries Served
7. Technical Support
8. Quotation Process
9. Contact Information

## Page-by-Page Changes

### Page 1 - Cover

- Replaced the broad headline "Advanced Water Filtration Solutions" with "Water Filtration Products and Engineering Support."
- Removed marketing-heavy supporting copy.
- Added the main product groups, Delhi and Noida locations, and Pan India and international supply coverage.
- Removed the website address from the cover.

### Page 2 - Company Introduction

- Replaced the previous trust-led marketing page with a factual company introduction.
- Added business scope, customer groups, supply coverage, MSME registration, office locations, phone, and email.
- Clarified the services provided from product identification through after-sales clarification.

### Page 3 - Why Choose ICONIC FILTRATION

- Replaced generic quality and trust statements with defined business responsibilities.
- Added application review, manufacturer data, quotation detail, quality inspection, delivery coordination, and after-sales support.
- Replaced the generic CTA with "Request Technical Consultation."

### Page 4 - Partner Brands

- Rewrote every partner description to state the actual product scope available in the catalogue.
- Removed broad partnership language.
- Added an ownership note for manufacturer logos and trademarks.
- Added a clear limitation for CHEMSEL because an approved product catalogue has not been supplied.

### Page 5 - Product Categories

- Replaced the four-card overview with seven defined product categories.
- Added FRP vessels, filter cartridges, and water-treatment accessories.
- Added a technical quotation-input note covering product, brand, application, flow, quantity, location, timeline, drawings, and BOQ.

### Page 6 - RO Membranes

- Replaced the short marketing description with a complete technical product page.
- Added product definition, uses, applications, advantages, Hydranautics brand reference, 4 inch and 8 inch formats, operating inputs, selection guidance, maintenance, related products, and quotation CTA.

### Page 7 - RO Pressure Vessels

- Replaced the general vessel description with technical information on membrane size, element count, pressure rating, port arrangement, connections, mounting, and maintenance.
- Added DAX brand reference, 4 inch and 8 inch sizes, 1 to 7 element configurations, and pressure options from 300 PSI to 1800 PSI.

### Page 8 - Dosing Pumps

- Replaced generic chemical-dosing copy with pump-selection information based on flow, pressure, chemical, turndown, suction condition, materials, and control signal.
- Added SEKO Tork, Kosmo, and Spring product families.
- Added maintenance guidance covering diaphragms, packing, valves, seals, strainers, calibration, and service kits.

### Page 9 - Ultra Filtration Systems

- Added a technical definition of ultrafiltration and its main design variables.
- Added FENTON UF 8585 information: 77 m2 membrane area, 0.03 um pore size, and 36-120 L/m2-h design flux.
- Added selection inputs, backwash and CIP guidance, applications, related products, and quotation CTA.

### Page 10 - FRP Vessels

- Added a new individual product page.
- Covered media volume, freeboard, service and backwash flow, working pressure, valve connection, distributor compatibility, and operating limits.
- Avoided naming an unverified brand.

### Page 11 - Filter Cartridges

- Added a new individual product page.
- Covered nominal and absolute ratings, differential pressure, material compatibility, end fittings, housing dimensions, and replacement practice.
- Added common 10, 20, 30 and 40 inch formats without assigning an unverified manufacturer.

### Page 12 - Water Treatment Accessories

- Added a new individual product page.
- Defined pressure-vessel parts, valves, vessel internals, instruments, sensors, controllers, dosing parts, antiscalant, resin, and maintenance items.
- Added DAX and SEKO references only where supported by available catalogue information.

### Page 13 - Industries Served

- Replaced general industry wording with sector-specific treatment applications.
- Added pharmaceutical, food and beverage, chemical, textile, power plants, hotels and hospitals, manufacturing, commercial buildings, and water-treatment plants.
- Avoided customer names, project claims, and unverified performance results.

### Page 14 - Technical Support

- Added a dedicated technical-input page for water data, hydraulic data, existing equipment data, drawings, commercial data, inspection, and documentation.
- Added factual support items: technical consultation, engineering guidance, product documentation, quality inspection, dispatch coordination, and after-sales support.
- Replaced the generic CTA with "Request Product Catalogue."

### Page 15 - Quotation Process and Contact

- Replaced the previous broad process summary with five specific steps.
- Added requirement review, product identification, quotation, inspection and dispatch, and after-sales support.
- Added the verified phone, email, Delhi office, and Noida branch office.
- Removed the website QR code and website address.

## Repeated and Generic Content Removed

- Removed repeated statements about confident decisions, quality products, responsive support, and long-term relationships.
- Removed repeated quotation and product-selection paragraphs.
- Removed broad claims that did not explain a business responsibility or technical requirement.
- Removed all website-related copy and QR links.
